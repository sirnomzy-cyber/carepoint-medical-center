FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

Have a question about care at CarePoint? We've gathered the most common ones here.

How do I book an appointment?
You can book online through our website 24/7, call our main line during office hours, or walk in for urgent care. Online booking gives instant confirmation.
Which insurance plans do you accept?
We accept most major insurance plans including BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealth, Medicare, and Medicaid. Call us to verify your specific plan.
Is emergency care available 24/7?
Yes. Our Emergency Department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, with on-call specialists available for critical situations at all times.
What are the consultation hours?
Regular clinics run Monday–Friday 8 AM–6 PM and Saturday 9 AM–3 PM. Emergency services are always open. Telehealth appointments are available evenings and weekends.
Can I do a virtual/telehealth consultation?
Absolutely. We offer secure video consultations for follow-ups, prescription renewals, and non-emergency assessments. Select "Telehealth" when booking online.
How do I access my medical records?
Patients can access records, lab results, and visit summaries through our secure patient portal. Request login credentials at reception or by emailing records@carepoint.com.
